# ADR-009: Resilience controls — load shedding, retry budget, fencing tokens, inbox
**Status:** Accepted
**Context:** The happy path was correct, but the brief's failures (5% payment failure, 2% duplicates, 30 s Order outage) plus normal production trouble (Redis failover, GC pauses, slow PSP) can push the sale into a retry storm, duplicate orders or a paused leader overwriting newer state. In our model, naive retries after a 2 s blip used 4.26× the load and never recovered (`resilience_sim.py`).
**Decision:** (1) `LoadShedder` rejects early with 503 + `Retry-After` when a request would miss its 300 ms deadline; waiting room admits at a measured rate. (2) Retries only for idempotent calls (same `Idempotency-Key`), exponential backoff with full jitter, `RetryBudget` of 10% of calls, max 3 attempts. (3) Leaders (expiry sweeper, outbox relay, reconciler) hold a Postgres `lease` with a monotonically increasing `fence_token`; every write checks `last_fence_token <= :token`. (4) Each consumer writes `inbox_message(consumer, message_id)` in the same TX as its business write. (5) Singleflight + TTL jitter on hot cache keys; 16 salted Redis sub-pools for the sale SKU.
**Alternatives:** Unlimited client retries (metastable); Redis/Redlock lease without DB fencing (a paused holder still writes); exactly-once Kafka transactions (don't cover side effects like SMS or the courier API); SERIALIZABLE isolation instead of row locks (retry storms on conflicts).
**Consequences:** + Blips stay blips: the controlled run recovered in the first second with 97.2% goodput; 0 duplicate orders; stale leaders can't write. − Some buyers get a fast 503 during overload; two extra tables (`inbox_message`, `lease`) and a purge job for the inbox; every leader write carries a token.
